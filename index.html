Ribbon cable? A 12-fiber ribbon can be treated like 12 fibers per tube (ribbon #1 = Blue, #2 = Orange, …).

Enter From and To (whole numbers 1–1728) to list fibers.

    Positions 13–24 repeat the 12 colors with 1 Black stripe, 25–36 with 2 stripes, and so on. Black units get a Yellow stripe instead. This applies to tubes 13+ and, at 24 per tube, to fibers 13–24.

    Stripe and dash conventions can vary by manufacturer — always check the cable spec sheet.
